# First principles and doctrinal foundations

These modules connect philosophical foundations to the existing human ontology and add central doctrines needed to understand the sacramental and theological graph. They contain 37 concepts and 81 relations, each with a Latin label or a cited rationale as appropriate. The encoding remains subject to scholarly review.

## First principles and truth

The **principle of noncontradiction** concerns the same thing at the same time and in the same respect. It connects to being and the understanding of first principles. The **first practical principle** concerns pursuing good and avoiding evil; it connects to synderesis and natural law. Aquinas places both in his account of first principles in [ST I–II, q. 94, a. 2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2094.htm#article2).

Truth has a general entry and two qualified senses: **truth in intellect** and **truth of things**. The former connects to intellectual judgment; the latter to being and divine intellect. Goodness and unity are likewise represented as transcendental considerations of being. Convertibility does not make the concepts synonymous. See [De veritate, q. 1, aa. 1–2](https://isidore.co/aquinas/english/QDdeVer1.htm), [ST I, q. 16](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1016.htm), [q. 5, a. 1](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1005.htm#article1), and [q. 11, a. 1](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1011.htm#article1).

**Essence** and **act of being** are distinguished in creatures. The corresponding relation to God expressly excludes received existence and composition. Analogical predication connects divine names with creaturely perfections without putting God inside a common univocal genus. See [De ente et essentia](https://isidore.co/aquinas/english/DeEnte&Essentia.htm), [ST I, q. 3, a. 4](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1003.htm#article4), and [q. 13, a. 5](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1013.htm#article5).

## Moral reasoning

**Synderesis is a habit; conscience is an act.** Their categories and edges preserve this difference. Conscience applies knowledge to conduct and directs the will; its errors require a separate assessment of voluntariness and negligence. Passion can impede a particular application without erasing first general principles. See [ST I, q. 79, aa. 12–13](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1079.htm#article12), [I–II, q. 19, aa. 5–6](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2019.htm#article5), and [q. 94, a. 6](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2094.htm#article6).

Natural law connects human practical reason to eternal law and the common good. Providence connects God to the ordering of created things while retaining contingent secondary causes. The image of God connects humans and angels through intellectual nature. These relations do not replace a moral deliberation model with a graph-distance score. Sources: [ST I–II, q. 91, aa. 1–2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2091.htm#article1), [q. 90, a. 2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/2090.htm#article2), [I, q. 22](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1022.htm), and [q. 93, aa. 4–6](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/1093.htm#article4).

## Christ, revelation, Church, and Eucharist

- **Hypostatic union:** one personal subject, the eternal Son, with divine and human natures. His created human will is distinguished from the divine will shared with the Father and Spirit. [ST III, q. 2](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4002.htm), [q. 18](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4018.htm), [CCC 475, 482](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P1J.HTM).
- **Saving work:** Passion connects to redemption and remission; Resurrection to justification and bodily resurrection. The efficient causality of resurrection extends to all; glorious exemplar conformity properly concerns the just. [ST III, q. 48](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4048.htm), [q. 56](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4056.htm).
- **Revelation:** Scripture and Tradition transmit the received revelation; the Magisterium serves and interprets the deposit. These offices do not create a new public revelation. [CCC 65–67](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__PH.HTM), [80–83](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__PL.HTM), [84–87](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__PM.HTM).
- **Church:** Christ as head, communion of saints, apostolic succession, and the four marks. Ecclesial communion is not identity with the divine essence. [CCC 781–810](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P28.HTM), [811–870](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P29.HTM).
- **Eucharist:** substantial presence, transubstantiation, and sacramental presence of the one sacrifice connect to the existing Eucharist, substance, accidents, Son, and Church nodes. The accidents relation applies specifically to the remaining Eucharistic species. [ST III, q. 75](https://www.newadvent.org/summa/4075.htm), [CCC 1362–1377](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P41.HTM).

## Computational meaning and remaining scope

First principles are recorded as authoritative propositions and conceptual constraints. This change does not implement a theorem prover, conscience evaluator, or supernatural transition model. A conditional edge must retain its qualification when used in explanations; a graph path alone does not establish a new conclusion.

This remains a selective ontology. Further substantial modules would include the commandments and Beatitudes, Christian prayer, covenant and salvation history, human law and social teaching, and fuller accounts of sacramental ministry and ecclesial authority. Those would need their own cited definitions and relations; a broad heading or a tour tag does not establish complete coverage.

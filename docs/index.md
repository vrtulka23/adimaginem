# adimaginem documentation

adimaginem explores Thomistic psychology and human ontology as a computational model for simulation. Aquinas's texts are authoritative for claims about his position; each computational rule should identify the passage and the interpretation behind it.

The project follows a [scholarly and Catholic method](method.md): claims about Aquinas, Catholic doctrine, and the simulation are identified and reviewed at their appropriate levels.
